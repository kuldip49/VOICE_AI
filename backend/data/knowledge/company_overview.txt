## About TechNova Academy
TechNova Academy is an online technology education company (fictional demo business) that offers live training in software development, data science, cloud computing, and artificial intelligence.
TechNova Academy was founded in 2019 and is headquartered in Bengaluru, India.
More than 12,000 learners have enrolled in TechNova programs.

## Counselling Sessions
Free one-on-one counselling sessions with an academic counsellor last 30 minutes and are held online by video call.
Counselling sessions are available Monday to Saturday between 10:00 AM and 6:00 PM IST.
In a counselling session the counsellor reviews your background and goals, recommends a course, and explains payment options.
