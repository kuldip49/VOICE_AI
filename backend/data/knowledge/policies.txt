## Refund Policy
Students can request a full (100%) refund within 7 days of their batch start date.
Between 8 and 14 days after the batch start date, a 50% refund is available.
No refunds are provided after 14 days from the batch start date.
Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method within 7-10 business days.
Refund requests must be sent to refunds@technova-academy.example.

## Payment Options
Payments can be made by UPI, debit card, credit card, or net banking.
No-cost EMI is available on credit cards and through our financing partner for courses priced above ₹25,000.
EMI eligibility is subject to approval by the financing partner.

## Discounts and Offers
Early-bird discount: 10% off the course price when enrolling at least 7 days before the batch start date.
Group discount: 15% off per person when 3 or more people enroll together in the same batch.
Discounts cannot be combined. Only one discount applies per enrollment.
Students and recent graduates (within 1 year) can apply for a merit scholarship of up to 20% after a short online assessment.

## Batch Transfer Policy
Students may transfer to a later batch of the same course once, free of charge, if requested before the 14th day of their current batch.

## Placement Assistance Policy
TechNova Academy provides placement assistance including resume reviews, mock interviews, and referrals to hiring partners for students who complete the course and capstone projects.
TechNova Academy does not guarantee job placement or any specific salary.

## Sales Policy
Counselling sessions are free and carry no obligation to enroll.
Sales representatives must not promise job guarantees, unapproved discounts, or certificates from third parties.
